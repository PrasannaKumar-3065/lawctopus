Who this is for

Most contract drafting courses teach you what a contract looks like. This one teaches you how to actually draft one — under pressure, for a real client.

Month by month

What you'll actually do

Every month is built around live drafting sessions, not just lectures. You watch, you try, you get feedback. By month six, you'll have drafted 24 agreements from scratch.

Month 1

Anatomy of a contract

Before you draft anything, you need to understand why every clause exists. You'll tear apart real contracts — finding the weaknesses, understanding what the definitions clause actually does, learning why a force majeure clause that sounds fine can fail you in court.

Definitions clause Indemnity Termination Boilerplate Conditions precedent
Month 2

Execution & your first freelance client

Stamp duty, registration, witness requirements — the unglamorous stuff that voids contracts when you get it wrong. You'll also draft your first NDA and employment agreement, then run through a live negotiation exercise. First freelancing session: setting up your Upwork profile.

NDA Employment agreement Software licensing Upwork profile
Month 3

International contracts

Governing law. Jurisdiction clauses. Arbitration rules. Cross-border contracts involve choices that can completely change how a dispute plays out — and most junior lawyers are guessing when they draft them. You won't be. Plus: loan agreements and advanced negotiation sessions.

International contracts Loan agreement E-contracts Governing law
Month 4

IP & the internet

Trademark licensing, patent licensing, copyright assignments, SaaS agreements, website terms and conditions, privacy policies, return & refund policies. Digital businesses need these constantly — and most lawyers don't know how to draft them well. You will.

Trademark licensing Copyright assignment SaaS agreement Privacy policy T&Cs
Month 5

Real estate agreements

Sale deeds. Leave and license agreements. Power of attorney. Franchisee agreements. Real estate work is bread and butter for a lot of lawyers — and a great source of freelance work. You'll draft each one live with the faculty and get feedback on your own version.

Sale deed Leave & license Power of attorney Franchisee agreement
Month 6

Corporate deals & career launch

Shareholders agreements. Share purchase agreements. JVA. Partnership deeds. The documents that show up on the most complex and highest-paying deals. By now you'll have a portfolio, an Upwork profile, and a career coaching session with LLS faculty. You graduate ready to earn.

Shareholders agreement Share purchase Joint venture Partnership deed
